
## 2026-10-02 collection (phase A worker)

Aliases: bare 570 is a common number, so queries.json is model_names_only with Bernina-qualified names (Bernina 570 QE, 570QE, B570, B 570 QE, B570QE, Bernina B 570, Bernina 570). Reddit searches rewritten brand-qualified; added r/Bernina, r/longarm, r/machinequilting. Dry run showed 54 threads; reviews:checks counts 53 Reddit threads; all have a 570 mention in the collected text. About 27 have the model in the title; the rest mention it in comments (some are Bernina-versus or dealer-advice threads where the 570 QE is one of several machines).

Maker documents: Bernina US product page (2017 and later model), support page, English manual PDF (196 pages, text layer, technical data p.164), US warranty page (search excerpt only), the before 2017 product page, and the 590 and 540 pages. The product brochure link points to a shared B 535 series brochure and was not used.

Maker documents disagree: weight is 24.2 lb on the page and 11.2 kg (25.35 lb) in the manual; our spec's 27 lb is a dealer figure. Page and manual swap height and depth in the dimensions row. Page lists BSR as an optional accessory; the manual supplied-accessory list has no BSR foot; the earlier model page says BSR included. The 24.2 lb, 8.5 in, 9 mm, 1,000 spm rows are identical on the 540, 570 QE and 590 pages (shared series table), so only the stitch counts, buttonholes, quilting stitches and price differ by model.

Maker does not publish: motor wattage (manual lists 90 W energy consumption only), throat in a manual table, price for the embroidery module, the meaning of the 12 beside the LED light, differential feed (Dual Feed is a different system). Warranty is the generic US Bernina warranty, not model-specific. Bernina US pages carry a Buy Now link to shop.berninausa.com; Stitch Check keeps the dealer-only treatment (rule 15).

Marketplaces: no marketplace listing, dealer-only. Amazon search returned no Bernina 570 QE listing. SMP dealer page ingested (1 review, rating 5). Other dealer pages (Meissner, sewingmachineshop, Village Sewing, SmarterFabric, University of Sewing) have no review platform the script can read. Two QuiltingBoard threads from the spec sources ingested as forum pages.

Collection: 54 Reddit sources (53 threads per checks), 702 items from Reddit, plus 2 forum pages and 1 dealer row = 705 items; 478 segments in 12 claim chunks. Ledger verified: retailer row (n 1, rating 5) and two forum rows present after collect:reddit.

Ambiguity: two machines named 570 QE exist (Type VIO current, Type LUN before 2017); owner posts rarely say which. Siblings 530, 540, 550, 590, 475, 770 and the 570 QE E (embroidery module) are tagged in editor.json; a post about the older machine cannot be separated by pattern.
