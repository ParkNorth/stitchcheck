## Unit / naming checks

- Model number vs anything it implies: 1634D is not a bigger machine than the 1034D. Forum owners describe the 1034D, 1634D and 1634DX as the same function at about the same price, differing in dial location, thread-path color coding and stitch fingers. "D" is Brother's differential feed suffix; "DX" is the refresh; "R1634D" is Brother's refurbished SKU.
- Throat measured needle-to-body or including the harp height? Not applicable; no throat figure published.
- Speed: 1,300 spm on Brother's page; review blogs repeat the same number.
- "Cover Stitch Serger" in one dealer listing title (mistersewvac.com) is wrong: Brother lists no coverstitch function.

## Spec conflicts

- Weight: 17.3 lb (review blogs) vs no Brother figure; the related 1034D is 13.88 lb at a dealer, so 17.3 lb may be a shipping weight. Catalog will use 17.3 lb flagged [verify].
- Dimensions: 12.5 x 13.5 x 15 in (review blog) vs nothing from Brother. Flagged [verify].
- Stitch count: 22 (sergerpro.com) vs Brother listing stitch types only. Catalog will show 22 as a blog figure.
- Threading: Brother says color-coded; a PatternReview owner (review 6163) says the 1634D lacks the color coding of the 1034D and has two stitch fingers instead of one. Listed, not resolved.
- Differential feed ratio: not published for the 1634D. Brother gives 0.7 to 2.0 for the 1034DX; do not carry across.

## Owner themes (paraphrase + attribute)

1. Plasticky but very simple to thread and set up; the small Brother sergers are called workhorses. (owner: https://sewing.patternreview.com/review/machine/6163)
2. One owner reports a rolled hem problem on the 1634D; another finds it as easy to use as the 1034D. (owner: https://sewing.patternreview.com/review/machine/5807)
3. Forum members treat the 1034D, 1634D and 1634DX as the same platform sold through different retailers at about the same price. (owners: https://sewing.patternreview.com/SewingDiscussions/topic/116994; https://sewing.patternreview.com/SewingDiscussions/topic/110523)
4. Only two PatternReview reviews exist for the 1634D against 66 for the 1034D. (https://sewing.patternreview.com/SewingMachine/1829)
