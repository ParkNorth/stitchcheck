## Unit / naming checks

- Model number vs anything it implies: "15R" is 15 in throat, R for regulated. Sibling models: 15M (manual, no regulation), 15 PRO (regulated, OLED touchscreen, 2,000 spm, discontinued and replaced by the 16X per graceframe.com). The 14+ was renamed 15R (leahday.com).
- Throat measured needle-to-body or including the harp height? Grace gives 15 in width and dealers give 8.5 in height as a workspace pair.
- Speed: 1,800 spm consistent across manufacturer and dealers.

## Spec conflicts

- Stitch regulation: graceframe.com says regulated; leahday.com review of the "14+ / 15R sit down" says no regulator and foot pedal control. The Leah Day page describes the sit down (table) configuration. Catalog will use: regulated on a frame, per Grace, with a note that sit down use is manual.
- Model status: 15 PRO discontinued, 16X named as replacement; 15R still purchasable. Catalog will label legacy, still sold.
- Price: bundle only ($5,198 with Q-Zone Queen; $6,000 used with Continuum King frame on forum.apqs.com). Machine only [verify].
- Class label: dealers say midarm, Grace's frame marketing and this guide treat 15 in as entry long-arm. Catalog will use long-arm with a boundary note.
