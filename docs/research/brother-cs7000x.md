## Unit / naming checks

- Model number vs anything it implies: CS7000X is the successor to the CS7000i; both list 70 stitches and used listings mix them. "70" in the model is not a stitch count claim by Brother, it is coincidence with the 70 stitches.
- Throat measured needle-to-body or including the harp height? Not published at all. Do not accept "large workspace" as a number.
- Speed: 750 spm in dealer listings; review blogs agree and call it the machine's main limit.
