## Juki

- Parent and country: JUKI Corporation, Japan, in business since 1938; industrial sewing machines from 1953. (https://www.jukihome.com/about-us)
- Series letters as used by Juki and dealers:
  - **HZL**: household or domestic sewing machines, computerized. "The HZL Series of computerized models has gained increased popularity for innovative features, excellent stitch quality and ease of operation." (https://www.jukihome.com/about-us; https://sewinginsight.com/juki-sewing-machines-and-serger-reviews/)
  - **TL**: "tailor usage" straight-stitch lockstitch machines, mechanical, "industrial feel". Models: TL-2000Qi, TL-2010Q, TL-2300 Sumato, TL-18QVP. Both TL-2000Qi and TL-2010Q are single-needle lockstitch, up to 1,500 spm, straight stitch only, 6 mm max stitch length, knee lifter. TL-2010Q adds slider speed control (200 to 1,500 spm), sub-tension lever and tension scale, and ships with 1/4 and 1/5 inch hopping feet. (https://www.worldweidner.com/pages/juki-tl-series-comparison-chart; https://forum.missouriquiltco.com/forum/we-don-t-know-much-but-we-know-quilters/general-discussion/76583-juki-tl2000qi-vs-juki-tl-2010q; http://kathyquiltsblog.blogspot.com/2011/03/juki-tl-2000qi-and-tl-2010q-compared.html)
  - **MO**: sergers ("small-size lock sewing machines"). MO-654DE is 2/3/4 thread, 1,500 spm, built-in rolled hem. MO-700 series is current; MO-735 is 2/3/4/5 thread with chainstitch, coverstitch and 5-thread safety stitch. (https://www.jukihome.com/about-us; https://jackmansfabrics.com/products/juki-mo-654de; https://www.allbrands.com/categories/267/printer_friendly)
  - **MCS**: coverstitch and chainstitch machines, MCS-1500 and MCS-1700. (https://www.jukihome.com/products/serging/coverstitch.html; https://jukijunkies.com/juki-mcs-1500-coverstitch-and-chainstitch-sewing-machine/)
  - **DDL**: industrial single-needle lockstitch line (garment plant machines). Meaning of the letters: not found. (https://www.goldstartool.com/juki-sewing-machines/)
- Warranty (US, home machines): "Juki America, Inc. warrants this Product for a period of five (5) years against defective materials and/or workmanship, two (2) years on motors, light assembly, wiring, switches, speed control and electrical components." Original purchaser only, not transferable, US and Canada service only. One dealer adds "90 days on adjustments". (https://carolinaforestvacuum.com/warranty-juki-america-inc/; https://www.qualitysewing.com/products/juki-tl-2010q-high-speed-sewing-and-quilting-machine)
- Distribution: "JUKI distributes sewing machines, accessories, parts, and other products through an authorized network of dealers. Warranty service is provided by the dealer from whom you purchased." Dealer locator lists "certified internet dealers". (https://www.jukihome.com/faq/; https://jukiquilting.com/dealers/dealer-locator.html/; https://www.jukihome.com/where-to-buy/)
- Prices seen (2025 to 2026 snippets): TL-2010Q sale $1,239 (regular $1,899); MO-654DE sale $549 (regular $1,399, retailer figure); HZL-F600 $629 to $1,000; HZL-DX7 about $1,400; TL-2000Qi $419.40 at one retailer. (https://www.qualitysewing.com/collections/juki; https://terracesewingmachine.com/products/Juki-HZL-F600-Full-Sized-Computer-Sewing-and-Quilting-Machine.html; https://www.quiltingboard.com/main-f1/juki-dx7-t278960.html)
- Conflict: TL-2000Qi at $419.40 is far below the TL-2010Q ($1,239 sale) while forum owners describe the gap as "about $50". Treat $419 as unverified (possibly a bundle or table listing) until the retailer page is read directly.
- Strongest at (from lineup facts): straight-stitch quilting and piecing (TL series, 1,500 spm, knee lifter), sergers (MO), coverstitch (MCS), and a large industrial catalog (DDL). Weaker at: entry-level mechanical zigzag machines and embroidery (not part of the covered series; embroidery out of scope).

## Janome

- Parent and country: Janome Sewing Machine Co., Ltd., Japan. Not re-verified from a primary page this session (Wikipedia blocked). US arm named in warranty text as Janome America, Inc. (https://www.janome.com/support/warranty/)
- Series:
  - **HD**: mechanical "heavy duty" machines: HD1000, HD3000, HD5000, HD9 Professional (HD9 is a 1,600 spm straight-stitch machine per Janome product page). (https://www.janome.com/product/hd9-professional/; https://www.kenssewingcenter.com/sewing-machines/janome-sewing-machines/janome-heavy-duty-sewing-machines.html)
  - **Memory Craft (MC)**: computerized sewing and quilting line, e.g. MC6700P. (https://www.janome.com/product/memory-craft-6700p/)
  - **Skyline**: computerized mid-range line: S3, S3AE, S5, S6AE, S7AE, S9. (https://www.kenssewingcenter.com/sewing-machines/janome-sewing-machines/janome-skyline-series-sewing-machines.html)
  - **CoverPro**: coverstitch line, e.g. CoverPro 3000 Professional. (https://www.qualitysewing.com/products/janome-coverpro-3000-professional)
  - **AirThread 2000D / 2000D Professional**: 2/3/4 thread serger with "One-Push Air Threading System", 1,300 spm, 2019 iF Design Award. Other sergers: MOD-8933 (3/4 thread), 6234XL (2/3/4 thread). (https://www.janome.com/product/airthread-2000d/; https://www.janome.com/janome-airthread-2000d-professional/; https://www.janome.com/product-category/machines/serger/)
- Warranty: "Janome warrants ... for twenty-five (25) years against defective materials and/or workmanship, five (5) years on electrical components, and one (1) year labor from the date of original purchase." Machines used commercially: 1 year parts and service. (https://cdn.sewingmachinesplus.com/media/products/Janome/Janome/Janome-warranty.html; https://www.janome.com/support/warranty/)
- Distribution: "Janome America, Inc. does not sell directly to the public via mail order or the Internet." Authorized dealers, some online, honor warranty. (https://www.janome.com/support/faqs/; https://sewing.patternreview.com/SewingDiscussions/topic/79008)
- Prices seen: HD1000 $399; HD3000 $599 to $699; HD5000 $499; HD9 Professional $1,699; MC6700P $2,499 to $3,799; Skyline and CoverPro 3000 prices: not found (dealers show "call for price"). (https://www.kenssewingcenter.com/sewing-machines/janome-sewing-machines/janome-heavy-duty-sewing-machines.html; https://www.michaels.com/product/janome-memory-craft-6700p-sewing-and-quilting-machine-564611990758375429)
- Strongest at: mechanical heavy-duty (HD line has four models), quilting-oriented computerized machines (MC6700P, Continental M8 with 13.5 in throat and ASR stitch regulator). Weaker at: published online pricing (dealer MAP hides much of it) and no direct online sales.

## Brother

- Parent and country: Brother Industries, Ltd., Japan; US subsidiary Brother International Corporation. Not re-verified from a primary page this session.
- Series:
  - **ST "Strong & Tough"**: ST371HD (37 stitches, nonstick foot, 6 feet), ST531HD (53 stitches, metal needle plate, finger guard), ST150HDH (50 stitches, LCD), ST4031HD serger. (https://www.brother-usa.com/products/st371hd; https://www.amazon.com/Brother-ST531HD-53-Hardcase-Machines/dp/B0D5QNF3FZ; https://www.amazon.com/Brother-ST150HDH-Stitches-Included-Off-White/dp/B0CTHL54KL)
  - **PQ**: high-speed straight-stitch quilting, PQ1500SL / PQ1600SL, 1,500 spm, wide table, 7 feet. (https://www.brother-usa.com/products/pq1500sl)
  - **CS**: computerized sewing, e.g. CS7000X (70 stitches, wide table, quilting feet). (https://www.amazon.com/Brother-Computerized-Quilting-Stitches-Off-White/dp/B09R3S9N9N)
  - **SE / PE**: sewing-and-embroidery and embroidery-only lines (SE700 4x4 in). Out of scope for Stitch Check; noted for lineup completeness only. (https://www.brother-usa.com/p/sewing-embroidery/SE700)
  - **1034D / 1034DX**: 3/4 thread sergers with differential feed, 1,300 spm, retractable knife, free arm. DX moves dials to the right side, LED light, larger trim trap, 7.0 kg vs 5.6 kg. (https://www.sewingmachinefun.com/brother-1034d-vs-1034dx/; https://sergerpro.com/brother-1034d-vs-1034dx-review/)
  - **2340CV**: dedicated coverstitch. (https://www.sewingmachinefun.com/brother-2340cv-review/)
- Warranty: Brother publishes a "25-Year Limited Warranty (for USA only)" statement. Breakdown per snippets conflicts: (a) 25 yr chassis casting, 5 yr electronic components and PCBs (replacement labor excluded), 1 yr parts, labor and accessories (serger statement); (b) 25 yr materials and workmanship, 2 yr electrical, 1 yr parts and labor; (c) PQ1500SL listing claiming 2 yr parts and labor, 6 yr electronics, 25 yr body. Catalog should use the brother-usa.com PDF once readable. (https://www.brother-usa.com/virdata/content/en-us/had/warrantydocuments/warrantystatements/sewingembroidery.pdf; https://help.brother-usa.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/54556/~/coverage-under-the-25-year-warranty; https://manuals.plus/brother/brother-serger-warranty-information; https://carolinaforestvacuum.com/warranty-brother-sewing-machines/)
- Distribution: sold online by Brother and mass retail (Amazon, Walmart, Best Buy). (https://www.bestbuy.com/product/brother-1034d-3-4-lay-in-thread-serger-with-lower-looper-threader/J7CJ33YHTS)
- Prices seen: 1034D $319.99; 1034DX $225 sale ($280 regular); CS7000X $279.99; ST371HD $539.99 (single listing, likely bundle; verify); ST150HDH $559.99; 2340CV $579.99; PQ1500SL not found. (https://threadworks.com/blogs/buying-guides/best-brother-sewing-machines-2026-every-model-decoded; https://www.walmart.com/c/kp/brother-1034d)
- Strongest at: entry sergers (1034D/DX repeatedly named the best-known starter serger), budget computerized machines, wide mass-retail availability. Weaker at: published warranty clarity (three different breakdowns in circulation) and no mid-priced 5-thread serger in the covered set.

## Singer

- Parent and country: SVP Worldwide, Nashville, Tennessee, USA; brands Singer, Husqvarna Viking, Pfaff; owned by Platinum Equity (controlling stake acquired 2021). (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SVP_Worldwide; https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/platinum-equity-completes-acquisition-of-global-sewing-machine-manufacturer-svp-worldwide-301345826.html)
- Series:
  - **Heavy Duty 44xx** (mechanical): 4411, 4423, 4432, 4452. All: metal interior frame, 1,100 spm, top drop-in bobbin, automatic needle threader. Differences are stitch count and accessory pack (4432 adds nonstick foot; 4452 adds walking foot, speed control per one source). "Heavy Duty" is a Singer series name, not a rating. (https://sewways.com/brands/singer-heavy-duty-comparison/; https://www.directsewing.co.nz/blogs/news/singer-heavy-duty-sewing-machines-nz-guide-4411-vs-4423-vs-4432-vs-4452)
  - **Heavy Duty 6xxx C** (computerized): 6800C, 586 stitch applications, LCD, metal frame, 6.4 in throat per Walmart title. (https://www.singer.com/products/singer-heavy-duty-6800c-sewing-machine; https://www.walmart.com/ip/Singer-6800C-Heavy-Duty-Computerized-Sewing-Machine/1255013631)
  - **Quantum Stylist 9960**: computerized, 600 stitches, 13 one-step buttonholes, extension table, 13 feet. (https://www.singer.com/products/singer-quantum-stylist-9960-sewing-machine)
  - **Professional 5 14T968DC**: 2/3/4/5 thread serger with coverstitch. **ProFinish 14CG754**: 2/3/4 thread serger. (https://www.qualitysewing.com/collections/singer-sewing-machines)
- Warranty: "twenty-five (25) years against defective materials and/or workmanship ... Two (2) years on motors, light assembly, wiring, switches, speed control and electrical components ... Ninety (90) days parts and labor." (https://www.sewingmachinesplus.com/singer4423.php; https://www.singer.com/products/singer-4423-heavy-duty-sewing-machine)
- Distribution: sells direct at singer.com and through mass retail.
- Prices seen: 4423 $85 (low, likely sale) ; 4432 $239.99; 4452 $219.99; 6800C $379.99 (regular $479.99); 9960 $549.99 (regular $689.99); 14T968DC $649.99 (regular $819.99); 14CG754 $159.95. (https://www.michaels.com/product/singer-4432-heavy-duty-sewing-machine-355321199899361297; https://villagesewing.com/products/singer-heavy-duty-6800c; https://www.qualitysewing.com/collections/singer-sewing-machines)
- Strongest at: lowest entry price for a metal-frame mechanical, and the only covered brand with a sub-$700 5-thread serger/coverstitch combo (14T968DC). Weaker at: warranty labor window (90 days vs 1 yr for Janome, Brother, Baby Lock) and no high-speed straight-stitch quilter in the covered set.

## Baby Lock

- Parent and country: Tacony Corporation, Fenton, Missouri, USA, family owned, founded 1946; introduced the Baby Lock, described as the first home serger, in the 1970s. (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tacony_Corporation; https://www.tacony.com/; https://www.referenceforbusiness.com/history/St-Th/Tacony-Corporation.html)
- Manufacturing note: sergers "mostly made in Japan", some parts marked Juki; sewing machines made in Taiwan, Vietnam, China, Thailand. Jubilant described as the same machine as Brother NS80e in different colors. (https://sewingmachineman.substack.com/p/brother-and-baby-lock; http://edgestitch.com/2018/02/where-is-this-sewing-machine-made/)
- Serger names (ascending price seen): Vibrant $524; Celebrate $1,049 sale ($1,352 regular); Victory $2,089; Acclaim $3,134; Accolade $3,499 sale ($4,499 regular); Triumph $5,249 sale ($6,214 regular); Imagine: in lineup, price not found. (https://www.kenssewingcenter.com/sergers/baby-lock-sergers.html; https://babylock.com/machines/serger; https://babylock.com/vibrant; https://babylock.com/celebrate; https://babylock.com/victory; https://babylock.com/acclaim)
- Sewing machines in scope: Jazz II (Jazz 2), 12 in to the right of the needle, "under $1,000" per retailer, exact price not found; Jubilant, 80 stitches, 6.25 in workspace, "around $500". (https://babylock.com/jazz-iid; https://www.qualitysewing.com/products/babylock-jazz-2-sewing-and-quilting-machine; https://www.qualitysewing.com/products/babylock-jubilant)
- Warranty: single snippet says sergers carry "a 25-year limited warranty, 10-year parts, 5-year electrical and 1-year labor." Confirm on babylock.com/warranty-policy (blocked this session). (https://babylock.com/warranty-policy; https://www.qualitysewing.com/products/baby-lock-serenity-serger)
- Distribution: "All Baby Lock machines and products are sold exclusively through Independent Authorized Baby Lock Retailers." Dealer-only confirmed. Note: sewingmachinesplus.com lists Baby Lock pages (see retailer file). (https://babylock.com/independent-retailers; https://babylock.com/retail-locator)
- Strongest at: sergers (seven-model ladder from ~$524 to ~$6,214, air threading on upper models). Weaker at: price transparency and no online direct purchase.

## Bernina

- Parent and country: BERNINA International AG, Steckborn, Switzerland; bernette is "a brand of Bernina International AG". (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernina_International; https://www.petoskeysewing.com/bernia)
- Series (number = tier; "the higher the number, the higher the specifications"):
  - **3 Series**: compact, 14.5 x 6.5 x 12 in; new B 325 / B 335 with DC motor, 4.3 in touch screen, adjustable presser foot pressure, semi-automatic threader. (https://www.bernina.com/en-US/Machines-US/Series-Overview/NEW-BERNINA-3-Series; https://www.maggieframes.com/blogs/embroidery-blogs/bernina-comparison-chart-decode-models-find-your-perfect-sewing-machine)
  - **4 Series**: ~20.9 lb, 16.7 x 7.9 x 12.9 in. (https://www.bernina.com/en-US/Machines-US/Series-Overview/BERNINA-4-Series)
  - **5 Series**: four models "for sewists, quilters and embroiderers" (e.g. 570 QE). (https://www.bernina.com/en-US/Machines-US/Series-Overview/NEW-BERNINA-5-Series)
  - **7 Series**: ~30.9 lb, 10 in right of needle; 9 mm stitch width on B 770 QE PLUS and B 790 PRO, 5.5 mm on B 735; automatic threading on B 790 PRO only. (https://www.bernina.com/en-US/Machines-US/Series-Overview/BERNINA-7-Series)
  - **8 / 9 Series**: 12 in right of needle (8 series); B 990 flagship. (https://www.maggieframes.com/blogs/embroidery-blogs/bernina-comparison-chart-decode-models-find-your-perfect-sewing-machine)
  - Suffixes: QE = Quilter's Edition, E = embroidery, PRO / PLUS = feature tiers (decode source: MaggieFrames chart; not verified against bernina.com this session).
  - **L 850**: 2/3/4 thread air-threading serger, 1,500 spm, $3,999 seen. **L 890**: 5 thread, adds chain and coverstitch, "true stitch automation". (https://shop.berninausa.com/overlocker/L-series/BERNINA-L-850; https://shop.berninausa.com/overlocker/L-series/BERNINA-L-890; https://www.qualitysewing.com/bernina-l-850.html)
  - **bernette**: sub-brand; b33/b35/b37/b38 sewing, Funlock 42/44/48 sergers and coverstitch, $299 to $1,299 seen. (https://www.petoskeysewing.com/bernia; https://shop.berninausa.com/bernette-machines)
- Warranty (US): "2 years for electrical parts, 5 years for printed circuit boards, and 20 years for mechanical parts from the date of purchase"; liability "limited to the cost of the affected part and does not include labor charges"; must register within 30 days or have dealer countersign card; service by non-authorized dealer voids it. bernette: refer to card in box. (https://www.worldweidner.com/pages/bernina-warranty; https://www.bernina.com/en-US/Support-US/BERNINA-Warranty)
- Distribution: dealer network with tiers ("Gold dealers ... 3 series, 5 series with embroidery, and 7 series without embroidery. Silver ... 3 and 5 series, Bronze only 3 series", forum report); shop.berninausa.com online store policies say returns and refunds go through local dealers. Dealer-only for BERNINA-badged machines is effectively confirmed; bernette appears on Amazon search pages but no official Amazon policy found. (https://shop.berninausa.com/terms-bernina; https://sewing.patternreview.com/SewingDiscussions/topic/102667; https://www.bernina.com/en-US/Where-to-Buy-US/Store-Locator)
- Prices seen (2025 to 2026): 335 $1,799; 435 $2,499; 570 QE $5,499; 770 QE PRO $10,999; 790 PRO $16,499; 990 $24,999; "prices set by BERNINA Dealer may vary". (https://sewsuitestudio.com/collections/bernina-sewing-quilting-machines; https://fabricbash.com/collections/bernina-sewing-machines; https://shop.berninausa.com/sewing-machines)
- Strongest at: top-tier quilting throat (10 to 12 in), BSR stitch regulation (not re-sourced this session), 20-year mechanical warranty, air-thread sergers. Weaker at: price (entry Bernina is ~$1,800), labor not covered under warranty, dealer-only access.
