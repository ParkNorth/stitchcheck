## Unit / naming checks

- Model number vs anything it implies: 570 QE is Quilter's Edition; 570 QE E adds the embroidery module. Two generations share the name: pre 2017 (5.5 mm width, 190 mm / 7.5 in freearm, 900 spm per bernina.com "before 2017" page) and current (9 mm, 8.5 in, 1,000 spm).
- Throat measured needle-to-body or including the harp height? Bernina states "right of the needle" width only. Height not published.
- Speed: 1,000 spm (current) vs 900 spm (older generation). Both appear in results; retailer copy often mixes them.

## Spec conflicts

- Generation: pre 2017 (5.5 mm, 7.5 in, 900 spm) vs current (9 mm, 8.5 in, 1,000 spm). Catalog will use current and footnote the older figures for used listings.
- Stitch count: 1,450 incl. alphabets (SMP) vs "up to 642 utility and decorative" (villagesewing.com) vs 240 (smarterfabric.com). Catalog will use 1,450 including alphabets, cited.
- Price: $5,499 MSRP (universityofsewing.com) vs $4,399 / $5,599 with module (robbreport.com launch coverage, older generation) vs about $4,500 (quiltingboard.com). Catalog will show $5,499 [verify], dealer only.
- BSR: integrated functionality, foot optional (bernina.com) vs bundles with and without. Catalog will list the foot as configuration dependent.
