## Owner themes (paraphrase + attribute)

1. Owners like getting a 5-thread serger and coverstitch in one box for roughly $450 to $650 and report clean stitches once set up. (owner: https://sewing.patternreview.com/review/machine/6636 ; https://sewing.patternreview.com/review/machine/6348)
2. In coverstitch mode there is little room under the foot; pressing the lifter bar further helps. Some report the right needle skipping and the triple cover never forming. (owner: https://sewing.patternreview.com/review/machine/6861)
3. Singer 2022 needles are required and hard to find, especially size 11; threading is difficult until learned. (owner: https://sewing.patternreview.com/SewingDiscussions/topic/79706)
