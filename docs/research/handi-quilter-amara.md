## Unit / naming checks

- "Amara 20" is a throat width: Handi Quilter states 20 in of throat space, and the spec article gives a 20.5 in wide by 9 in high sewing opening. The Amara 24 is the renamed Forte 24 (handiquilter.com/product/hq-amara-24/); the Amara ST is the sit down version (handiquilter.com/product/amara-st/). This entry is the Amara 20 stand up only.
- Throat measured needle-to-body: 20 in width; 9 in height from the sewing opening figure. Usable depth per pass on the frame is less because of the take up roller; no figure published.
- Speed: 2,500 spm is the manual mode maximum on the manufacturer spec article; dealers repeat "up to 2,500 spm". No regulated cruise range was published.

## Owner themes (paraphrase + attribute)

1. Seven months in, an owner who traded a Bernina Q20 sit down for an Amara on a 12 ft frame has no regrets; free motion and ruler work both easy; online videos solved most questions. (owner: https://sewing.patternreview.com/SewingDiscussions/topic/127484)
2. Moving up from a Moxie, the head is noticeably heavier and caused arm fatigue for the first few quilts before endurance built. (owner blog: https://www.stringandstory.com/blog/hqamara)
3. Five verified owner reviews aggregated at 4.75 of 5; price is the recurring hesitation. (aggregator: https://craftyolo.com/handi-quilter-amara-review/)
