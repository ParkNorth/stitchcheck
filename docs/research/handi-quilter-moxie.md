## Unit / naming checks

- Model number vs anything it implies: "Moxie" carries no number. The Moxie XL is a separate 18 in machine (handiquilter.com/product/moxie-xl/). The Moxie ST is the sit down version.
- Throat measured needle-to-body or including the harp height? Handi Quilter states 15 in throat space; height is not given in the snippets found. Treat 15 in as needle to body width.
- Speed: manufacturer max 1,800 spm; the manual's 50 to 800 spm figure is the regulated cruise range, not the mechanical max. Show both.

## Owner themes (paraphrase + attribute)

1. Still happy after a year, but tension changes take a moment to settle and moving the head after stitching can snap the top thread and force a rethread. (owner: https://www.stringandstory.com/blog/handiquiltermoxiereview2)
2. Tension, thread breaking and fabric bunching are the standing troubleshooting topics; retiming requires a Handi Quilter retailer. (guide: https://quiltinginfo.com/handi-quilter-moxie-troubleshooting/)
3. Handi Quilter's own support articles document tension and thread shredding checklists, suggesting they are routine setup issues. (manufacturer support: https://support.handiquilter.com/hc/en-us/articles/115001348752-Thread-shredding-or-breaking)
