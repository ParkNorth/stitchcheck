## Shipping policy

- Free shipping in the contiguous 48 states on orders over $49. Not eligible: Hawaii, Alaska, Canada, APO/FPO/DPO, and "large or heavy items that require special shipping methods such as assembled industrial sewing machines and some sewing cabinets". (https://sewingmachinesplus.com/pages/processing-shipping)
- Weekday orders before 2 pm Pacific usually ship same day; orders after 3 pm Friday, Saturday or Sunday process Monday; no weekend or holiday processing. (same)
- A "Free 3-Day Shipping" promotional page exists for selected machines. (https://www.sewingmachinesplus.com/3day.php)

## Price match

- "100% Guaranteed Price Match": will beat, not only match, a lower price, when the competitor is an authorized dealer of the product selling a new, in-stock, non-auction, warranted item at or above manufacturer wholesale cost. Honored up to 30 days after purchase as in-store credit for the difference. Claim by phone (800) 401-8151. (https://sewingmachinesplus.com/pages/sewing-machines-plus-store-policies; https://www.sewingmachinesplus.com/customer-service.php)

## Authorized-dealer statements and brands carried

- Site copy: "proud authorized dealers for Bernina, Brother, Juki, Baby Lock, Handi Quilter, Husqvarna Viking, Janome, Pfaff, Singer, Bernette, Grace Company, Consew and many more." Title tag: "50+ year Authorized Dealer". (https://sewingmachinesplus.com/; https://sewingmachinesplus.com/pages/sewing-machines)
- Brand pages exist for Juki (https://sewingmachinesplus.com/pages/juki-brands), Baby Lock (https://sewingmachinesplus.com/pages/baby-lock-brands; https://sewingmachinesplus.com/collections/babylock-sewing-machines) and Bernina (https://sewingmachinesplus.com/pages/bernina-brands).
- Product listings seen for all four core brands: Juki TL-2010Q (https://www.sewingmachinesplus.com/juki-2010q-machine.php), Juki MO-654DE (https://www.sewingmachinesplus.com/juki-MO-654DE.php), Janome HD3000 (https://sewingmachinesplus.com/products/janome-hd3000), Brother 1034D (https://www.sewingmachinesplus.com/brother-1034d-sergermachine.php), Singer 4423 (https://www.sewingmachinesplus.com/singer4423.php), Baby Lock Jubilant (https://sewingmachinesplus.com/products/bl-jubilant), Baby Lock Anna (https://www.sewingmachinesplus.com/babylock-anna.php).
- Juki notes SMP as a "JUKI National Account Dealer" in a Juki blog post. (https://sewinwithjuki.com/2025/05/10/meet-your-local-juki-national-account-dealer/)
- Brands NOT carried: none of the six target brands is absent. The task assumption that Baby Lock and Bernina are missing is not supported; both have brand pages and product listings. Whether Baby Lock and Bernina machines can be added to cart and shipped (vs in-store only, given both brands' dealer-only rules) is not found; check a listing directly.
