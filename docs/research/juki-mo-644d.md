## Unit / naming checks

- Model number vs anything it implies: "644D" is a catalog code; "Garnet Line" is a dealer tier name, not a grade. Series recorded as MO per the Juki letter code. Ken's title says "3/4 Thread" because the 2-thread converter is optional on this model.
- Throat: n/a.
- Speed: 1,500 spm from dealer spec; Juki page value not captured in snippet.
- Weight: kg is the published figure; lb is the dealer rounding.

## Owner themes (paraphrase + attribute)

1. Least finicky serger the owner has used; easy tension, length and differential adjustments; fabric feeds straight. (owner: https://sewing.patternreview.com/review/machine/5209)
2. Lower looper threading is the recurring complaint; no air threading but not missed by owners with good eyesight. (owner: https://sewing.patternreview.com/review/machine/5866)
3. Versus 654DE: 644D has left-side differential dial, stitch length inside the door, no lower looper lever, converter sold separately, aimed at light to medium fabric. (owner: https://sewing.patternreview.com/SewingDiscussions/topic/95466)
4. First-serger shoppers comparing 644D, 654DE and Brother 1034D are steered to the 654DE if the budget stretches. (owner: https://sewing.patternreview.com/SewingDiscussions/topic/121187)
5. Further owner reviews: https://sewing.patternreview.com/review/machine/6667

## Cross-shop set

1. `juki-mo-654de`: the next model up with converter included and external dials.
2. `brother-1034d`: the cheaper 3/4-thread alternative with a free arm.
3. `babylock-vibrant`: another 2/3/4 thread manual-threading serger in the same tier, dealer sold.

## Editorial angle

For a first-serger buyer deciding whether the 644D's lower price is worth giving up the 654DE's converter and outside dials.
