## Unit / naming checks

- "Sweet Sixteen" is a throat width: the manual gives 16 in wide by 8.25 in high. By the published tier bands 16 in is mid-arm, and it is a sit down machine, not frame mounted; Handi Quilter and dealers market it as a sit down longarm. Not to be confused with the HQ Sixteen (frame) or Simply Sixteen (frame, current).
- Throat measured needle-to-body: yes, 16 x 8.25 in from the manual.
- Speed: manual 1,500 spm max vs dealer 1,800 spm. Use the manual.

- Speed: 1,500 spm (manual) vs 1,800 spm (rockymountainsewing.com). Catalog will use 1,500 because the manual is the manufacturer document.
- Table height: 26 to 42 in (rockymountainsewing.com) vs 27 to 40 in (SMP table listing). Both shown.
- Status: rockymountainsewing.com says discontinued and points to the Moxie ST; handiquilter.com/hq-capri/ positions the Capri as the Sweet Sixteen's successor in footprint and the Capri is itself retired. ## Owner themes (paraphrase + attribute)

1. Sit down owners chose it to avoid the back strain of a frame machine and prefer moving fabric; several report no service since dealer setup. (owner: https://www.quiltingboard.com/main-f1/sit-down-sweet-16-experiences-thoughts-t299737.html)
2. Prewound bobbins upset tension; technicians agree; adjusting tension yourself is a learned skill. (owner: https://www.quiltingboard.com/main-f1/opinions-handi-quilter-sweet-16-sit-down-machine-t138579-2.html)
3. Long term owner: a joy to use, but 60 x 80 in quilts create real drag on a table, and owners who expect zero adjustment across battings and threads will be unhappy. (owner blog: https://www.savoreverystitch.com/2017/01/handi-quilter-sweet-16-review.html)
4. Six months in, works great and easy to use; the stitch counter helps with billing. (owner: https://forum.missouriquiltco.com/forum/we-don-t-know-much-but-we-know-quilters/general-discussion/16023-hq-sweet-sixteen-reviews)
5. A PatternReview thread titled "HQ Sweet 16 issue" exists; contents not visible in snippets. (https://sewing.patternreview.com/SewingDiscussions/topic/111801)
