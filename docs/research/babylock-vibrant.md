## Unit / naming checks

- Model number vs anything it implies: Vibrant = BL460B. BLS1 = Celebrate. The two are adjacent in Baby Lock's line and are often confused in bundles and used listings. "Genuine Collection" is Baby Lock's series label for its entry machines.
- Throat: not published.
- Speed: dealer sheets 1,500 spm; an early snippet cluster mentioned 1,300 spm without attribution. Use 1,500 flagged [verify].

## Manufacturer claims (attribute, do not assert)

- "Easy color-coded threading chart" (manufacturer claim, product page)
- "Perfect overlocker machine for beginners" (dealer claim, listing)
- Dealers say the Vibrant is mechanically the same as Husqvarna H Class 200/250S, built by Maruzen/Jaguar (dealer forum claim, not confirmed by Baby Lock).

## Spec conflicts

- Model number: BLS1 (as briefed) vs BL460B (Baby Lock). Catalog will list BL460B and explain.
- Price: $499 regular (most dealers) vs $799 regular (some). Catalog will show $499 labeled dealer-set.
- Speed: 1,500 spm (dealer sheets) vs 1,300 spm (unattributed snippet). Catalog will use 1,500 flagged [verify].

## Owner themes (paraphrase + attribute)

1. Simple operation, consistent stitches and a front that opens for threading and cleaning. (owner: https://sewing.patternreview.com/review/machine/7052)
2. A 2026 review reports the upper looper scraping across and denting the needles, needing service. (owner: https://sewing.patternreview.com/review/machine/7653)
3. Buyers upgrading from older manual sergers find the tension dials confusing and often end up wanting air threading. (owner: https://sewing.patternreview.com/SewingDiscussions/topic/120366)
