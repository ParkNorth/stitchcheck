## Unit / naming checks

- Model number: BLS3. "Victory" implies nothing about thread count or stitch count; it is a 4/3/2 thread overlock with no coverstitch and no chain stitch. Baby Lock's serging comparison chart (babylock.com/serging-comparison-charts) is the page to confirm the tier.
- Not to be confused with the Celebrate (manual threading sibling with tension dials), the Acclaim or Accolade (step ups with more stitches), or the Imagine (BLE1AT-2, the model it replaced).
- Throat: n/a for a serger.
- Speed: 1,500 spm appears on dealer pages; Baby Lock's own spec sheet was not opened. Retailer copy and dealer copy agree.
- Differential feed is quoted as ratios "2 to 1" and "3 to 4" in dealer copy, not as a 0.6 to 2.0 range as on the Imagine. Do not convert one into the other.

## Owner themes (paraphrase + attribute)

1. Consistent stitching out of the box; air threading and auto tension praised; paid about $1,200. (owner: https://sewing.patternreview.com/review/machine/7374)
2. Still recommended three years after a $1,500 purchase; minor gripe that the machine opens on the right only so the needle threader must be held left handed. (owner: https://sewing.patternreview.com/review/machine/7531)
3. Used Imagine near $1,100 vs new Victory near $2,000: Victory described as an updated Imagine with LEDs and higher foot lift; a 2003 Imagine owner would buy either again. (owner discussion: https://sewing.patternreview.com/SewingDiscussions/topic/117409)
4. Celebrate vs Victory: one poster preferred the Celebrate's slow speed control on curves; the Victory wins on no tension adjustment. (owner discussion: https://sewing.patternreview.com/SewingDiscussions/topic/128523)
5. No trouble reported after purchase on a shop owner's recommendation; posters estimate price above $1,500. (owner discussion: https://sewing.patternreview.com/SewingDiscussions/topic/114042)
6. Further threads exist comparing the Victory with the Acclaim (topic 115471) and Accolade (topic 116286); contents not visible in snippets.
