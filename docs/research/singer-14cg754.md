## Unit / naming checks

- Model number vs anything it implies: "ProFinish" and "Commercial Grade" are Singer marketing names; the 14CG754 is a household 2/3/4-thread serger. "14CG754" does not encode thread count or speed. It is not part of the Heavy Duty line; the 14HD854 is.
- Throat measured needle-to-body or including the harp height? Not applicable.
- Speed: manufacturer max 1,300 spm; retailer copy matches.
- Differential feed "0.7 to 2.0 mm" in Amazon copy is a ratio, not millimeters. Recorded as a ratio.

## Owner themes (paraphrase + attribute)

1. Threading is fine with the diagram: an owner who paid $199 recommends it and says threading is fast once the instructions are followed. (owner: https://sewing.patternreview.com/review/machine/2271)
2. Value and stitch quality: other PatternReview owners praise stitch quality, speed and motor for the price; dim lighting is the recurring complaint. (owner: https://sewing.patternreview.com/review/machine/3143; https://sewing.patternreview.com/review/machine/5369)
3. Not for everyone: one owner found it very hard to thread and could not get a rolled hem; a discussion thread has shoppers put off by bad reviews. (owner: https://sewing.patternreview.com/SewingDiscussions/topic/23518)
4. Versus Heavy Duty: a PatternReview thread comparing ProFinish and Heavy Duty sergers frames the 14HD854 as the same platform with a bigger knife and stronger motor. (owner: https://sewing.patternreview.com/SewingDiscussions/topic/114880)
