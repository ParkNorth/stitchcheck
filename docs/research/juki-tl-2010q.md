## Unit / naming checks

- Model number vs anything it implies: "TL-2010Q" is not a 20 in arm. Arm is 8.5 in (SMP) or "9 in" (dealer titles). "Long-Arm" in SMP's title is retailer language.
- Throat measured needle-to-body or including the harp height? SMP says 8.5 in throat; dealers say 9 x 6 in, which reads as width x height. Not confirmed which is needle-to-pillar.
- Speed: manufacturer max vs retailer copy: 1,500 spm appears in dealer copy attributed to Juki; jukihome.com snippet did not show the number. Stitch length max 6 mm (SMP).

## Spec conflicts

- Throat: 8.5 in (SMP) vs 9 in x 6 in (Carolina Forest Vac and Sew, Tops Vacuum and Sewing). Catalog will use 8.5 in with [verify] because it is the more specific figure and matches the sibling TL-2000Qi's "9 in" being a rounded dealer number.
- Price: $900 (SMP show model) vs $1,199 (Lowe's) vs a 2017 SMP liquidation snippet showing "regular price $2,099" that appears to be a frame bundle. Catalog will use $1,199 for a new unit and flag SMP for direct check.
- Included feet: sewmuchmoore.com lists four feet; SMP and MSQC forum posters say the 1/4 and 1/5 hopping feet ship with the 2010Q and not the 2000Qi. Catalog lists both sets as "dealer reported".
- Weight: 25.4 lb (Rocky Mountain Sewing, Premier Stitching). No conflicting figure found.

## Owner themes (paraphrase + attribute)

1. 2010Q vs 2000Qi: forum members say the 2010Q adds the front speed slider, a pre-tensioner disc and the 1/4 and 1/5 hopping feet, otherwise the same head at a higher price. (owner: https://forum.missouriquiltco.com/forum/we-don-t-know-much-but-we-know-quilters/general-discussion/76583-juki-tl2000qi-vs-juki-tl-2010q ; also http://kathyquiltsblog.blogspot.com/2011/03/juki-tl-2000qi-and-tl-2010q-compared.html)
2. Portable vs true industrial: a PatternReview thread weighs the 2010Q against a table-mounted industrial; the portable footprint decides it for home sewists. (owner: https://sewing.patternreview.com/SewingDiscussions/topic/70727)
3. Marketplace reviews: sturdy, user friendly, speed control and thread cutter praised; one owner reports ongoing tension trouble; regular user oiling is required and explained in the manual. (owner: https://www.ebay.com/itm/152121198975 review block)

## Cross-shop set

1. `juki-tl-2000qi`: same head, bobbin and needle; buyers decide whether the slider, pre-tensioner and two feet are worth the premium.
2. `juki-ddl-8700`: buyers with floor space and a straight-stitch-only workload price the true industrial with servo motor and table.
3. `janome-mc6650`: quilters who want a 10 in arm but also need zigzag, buttonholes and a computerized interface.
