## Unit / naming checks

- Model number vs anything it implies: "F600" is a tier number; "Quilt Pro Special" and "Exceed" are Juki series names. 225 stitches does not imply a larger machine than the F300, which shares the chassis and 900 spm.
- Throat measured needle-to-body or including the harp height? Dealers give 8 in to the right of the needle; the owner measurement of 7.75 in suggests the 8 is rounded. Standard arm, not mid-arm.
- Speed: 900 spm on Juki and dealers alike.
- Stitch count: one SMP snippet says 255; Juki says 225.

## Cross-shop set

1. `juki-hzl-f300`: same chassis, 106 stitches, fewer accessories, lower price.
2. `juki-tl-2010q`: 1,500 spm straight stitch for quilters who do not need patterns.
3. `janome-mc6650`: the mid-price computerized quilting machine with a 10 in arm.

## Editorial angle

For a quilter or garment sewer choosing between the F600's complete kit and either the cheaper F300 or a longer-arm machine.
