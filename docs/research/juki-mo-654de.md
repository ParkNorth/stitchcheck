## Unit / naming checks

- Model number vs anything it implies: "654DE" is a catalog code; "Pearl Line" is Juki's series name for household sergers, not a grade. "DE" does not denote differential and electronic in any published Juki text found.
- Throat: not published.
- Speed: Juki 1,500 spm; dealer copy matches.

## Manufacturer claims (attribute, do not assert)

- "Performs all the popular overlock and flatlock variations" (manufacturer claim, product page)
- "Lightweight enough for portability" (dealer copy)
- "Industrial-strength" appears in blog comparisons, not in Juki text.

## Spec conflicts

- Price: $399.99 vs $499.00 at the same retailer. Catalog will use $399.99 labeled dealer-variable.
- Weight: dealer "just over 15 pounds" vs no Juki figure captured. Catalog will use 15.0 lb flagged [verify].
- One snippet listed a "5-thread safety stitch" for this model; a 2-needle, 4-thread machine cannot form it. Treated as a snippet error and excluded.

## Owner themes (paraphrase + attribute)

1. Owners who have used both say the Juki is sturdier than the Brother 1034D and easier to fix yourself, at a higher price. (owner: https://sewing.patternreview.com/SewingDiscussions/topic/118297)
2. Threading is a little challenging for new sewers but the front panel opens wide for looper access. (owner/blog: https://blog.closetcorepatterns.com/serger-review-the-juki-mo654de/)
3. Versus the MO-644D, the 654DE puts the differential and length dials outside the door and handles thicker layers; the 644D needs a separate 2-thread converter. (owner: https://sewing.patternreview.com/SewingDiscussions/topic/95466)

## Cross-shop set

1. `brother-1034d`: cheaper 3/4-thread alternative; savings vs 2-thread and build.
2. `juki-mo-1000`: same brand with jet air looper threading at roughly three times the price.
3. `babylock-vibrant`: another 2/3/4 thread, 1,500 spm manual-threading serger sold through dealers.

## Editorial angle

For a buyer choosing between the cheapest serger and the first machine that adds 2-thread stitches and a heavier build, and asking whether the Juki premium is worth it without air threading.
