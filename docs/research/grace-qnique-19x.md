## Unit / naming checks

- "19X" is a throat width: Grace states 19 in of throat space. It is 1 in short of the 20 in Amara and below the published 18 in long-arm floor by throat, though marketed as a longarm; the catalog calls it an entry long-arm on a frame.
- Rename: Grace's product pages now read "Grace 19X" and "Grace 19X Elite"; dealers and SMP still say Q'nique 19X. Same machine.
- 19X vs 19X Elite: different motor (2,000 vs 2,600 spm), screen (OLED vs 7 in), dimmable LEDs and built in bobbin winder on the Elite. Do not mix Elite figures into the 19X table.
- Throat measured needle-to-body: 19 in width; height not published.
- Speed: manufacturer 2,000 spm for the 19X; dealer copy "2,000 stitches in 60 seconds" is the same number.

## Owner themes (paraphrase + attribute)

1. Owner who just set up a 19X Elite on a 12 ft frame found it lighter to move for feathers and circles; another poster relays that in the Quilting with Grace group the 19 owners are happier than non-Pro 15 owners. (owner: https://www.quiltingboard.com/main-f1/qnique-19-long-arm-machine-t310290.html)
2. Three years in, still loves it; Grace technicians are helpful by phone. (owner: https://forum.missouriquiltco.com/forum/we-don-t-know-much-but-we-know-quilters/general-discussion/76114-grace-q-nique-long-arm)
3. Thread cutter firing on its own every few seconds and breaking needles; fix path was regulator and encoder diagnostics with Grace. (owner: https://www.quiltingboard.com/main-f1/having-problems-stitch-regulator-thread-cutter-t298173.html)
4. Moving the carriage too fast causes thread breaks and skipped stitches; tension checklist before calling Grace. (blog guide: https://freemotionproject.com/2019/03/troubleshoot-tension-issues-on-a-grace-qnique-longarm/)
5. Manufacturer site reviews are uniformly positive ("my dream come true", "easy to set up"); treated as positioning, not owner evidence. (https://graceframe.com/en/product/grace-19x-elite-quilting-machine)
