## Unit / naming checks

- Model number vs anything it implies: "F300" is the lower tier of the same chassis as the F600; "Exceed" and "Home Deco" are series names. An Amazon bundle title says "9 inch Throat"; dealer spec sheets say 8 in. Use 8.
- Throat measured needle-to-body or including the harp height? 8.0 in right of the needle, 4.4 in high per dealer sheet.
- Speed: 900 spm, same as the F600.

## Manufacturer claims (attribute, do not assert)

- "Sews through thick layers like butter" (owner phrasing, PatternReview)
