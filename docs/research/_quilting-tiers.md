## 1. Throat space: what is being measured

- Throat space (also harp or arm space) is the open area between the needle and the machine body to the right of the needle; it sets how much quilt you can work before repositioning or rolling. (https://sewvanta.com/what-is-throat-space-quilting/)
- Manufacturers quote width first; some also give height. Examples: Brother PQ1600S "5.7 x 8.7 in needle-to-arm space" (https://www.brother-usa.com/products/pq1600s); Grace Q'nique 15R "total workspace 8.5 x 15 in" (https://www.premierstitching.com/grace-qnique-15r-midarm-quilting-machine-hoop-frame-pro-frame-combo/); Bernina 570 QE "8.5 in right of the needle" (https://www.sewingmachinesplus.com/bernina-570qe.php).
- On a frame, usable quilting depth per pass is less than the throat width because the take up roller and clamps occupy part of it. No manufacturer figure for usable depth was found; state this as a caution, not a number.

| Long-arm | 18 to 30 in; most common 20, 24, 26 in | https://sewvanta.com/mid-arm-vs-longarm-machines/ |

Boundary cases the guide must handle:
- 15 in frame machines (Handi Quilter Moxie, Grace Q'nique 15R) fall inside the published mid-arm band, yet Handi Quilter markets the Moxie as a "longarm" (https://handiquilter.com/product/hq-moxie-15-quilt-machine/) and Grace dealers call the 15R a "midarm" (https://www.premierstitching.com/grace-qnique-15r-midarm-quilting-machine-hoop-frame-pro-frame-combo/). The guide will call these entry long-arms by function (frame mounted, stitch regulated) and note the throat sits in the mid-arm band.
- Juki names its 8.5 in TL machines "long arm" in some retailer titles (https://www.sewingmachinesplus.com/juki-2010q-machine.php). By throat they are domestic.
- Sit down machines with 16 to 18 in throats (HQ Sweet Sixteen, Juki Miyabi J-350QVP) are mid-arm by throat and are not frame mounted.

## 5. Room footprint

- Handi Quilter: a 10 ft frame wants roughly 12 to 13 ft by 7.5 to 8 ft of floor (https://handiquilter.com/how-much-space-do-i-need-for-my-new-longarm/).
- Handi Quilter, Moxie specific: the frame and machine occupy about 4.5 ft by 8 ft (or 10 ft), and Handi Quilter shades a larger area for moving around the frame while advancing the quilt (https://handiquilter.com/space-moxie-needs/). The Moxie frame is height adjustable 33 to 44 in.
- Dealer guide: a 10 ft frame needs 12 by 8 ft and a 12 ft frame needs 14 by 8 ft; leave 2 ft open on every side for the head and a walking path; one long side can go against a wall, reducing width to about 10 to 12 ft (https://rockymountainsewing.com/longarm-quilting-frame-size-guide/).
- Small room options: Handi Quilter Little Foot 5 ft frame for the Moxie (https://handiquilter.com/product/hq-moxie-15-quilt-machine/); Grace Q-Zone Hoop at 54 in wide, quilting crib to king by repositioning (https://www.sewingpartsonline.com/products/q-zone-hoop-frame-grace-company).
- Sit down mid-arms and domestics need a table footprint only; no published room figure found.

## 6. Price bands by tier (summary; full sourced table in _long-arm-cost.md)

- Domestic straight stitch quilters: about $580 to $1,900 (Brother PQ1600S to Juki TL-18QVP).
- Sit down mid-arms: about $6,000 to $9,000 (Juki Miyabi sale, HQ Sweet Sixteen, HQ Capri).
- Entry frame long-arms 15 to 19 in with frame: about $5,000 to $7,600 (Q'nique 15R bundle, Moxie MSRP, Q'nique 19X).
- 20 in and up systems: about $12,500 to $52,999 (HQ Amara 20 to Gammill Statler 26).
- All figures are the seen prices in _long-arm-cost.md with their URLs; none are averages.

## 7. Wording rules for the guide

- Quote "longarm" and "midarm" as the seller's label when the throat does not match the published band.
- Give throat as width, and height when published. Never infer height.
- Give max spm as the manufacturer's motor figure and, when available, the regulated cruise range beside it.
- State frame inclusion on every long-arm price.
