## 9. Delivery, setup, training

- Gammill: pricing is all inclusive of delivery, installation and new owner training in the lower 48 (https://gammill.com/machines/buyers-guide-2/pricing/).
- APQS: top model quoted as "plus shipping" in one guide (https://craftanddesign.net/longarm-quilting-machine-prices/); dealer pages do not state freight.
- Handi Quilter and Grace: no published delivery or setup fee found; online Grace dealers advertise free shipping on some bundles (https://www.sewvacdirect.com/grace-qnique-15r-midarm-quilting-machine-with-q-zone-queen-frame-with-bonus-bundle/). Frames ship flat for owner assembly.

## 10. Consumables to budget

- M class bobbins: Moxie and Q'nique 15R (https://www.qualitysewing.com/products/moxie-quilting-machine-handi-quilter; https://www.premierstitching.com/grace-qnique-15r-midarm-quilting-machine-hoop-frame-pro-frame-combo/).
- 134 system needles: Moxie (https://support.handiquilter.com/hc/en-us/articles/360049689372-Handi-Quilter-Quilting-Machine-Needles); Q'nique 135x5 / DPx5 with 134 listed as equivalent (Grace 15R instruction PDF).
- Domestic TL class uses HAx1 (130/705H) needles and L class TL bobbins (https://www.qualitysewing.com/juki-tl-18qvp-haruka-high-speed-sewing-and-quilting-machine.html).
- Prices for needles and bobbins were not collected.
