## Unit / naming checks

- Model number vs anything it implies: "1008" is a Classic Series number, not a stitch count or speed. "1008S" is the portable variant with a built-in handle (16 stitches per one UK listing). "1008N" on the Sewing Machines Plus listing is a market suffix. "Heavy Duty" in used-dealer titles is a listing word, not a Bernina rating.
- Throat measured needle-to-body or including the harp height? Not published.
- Speed: 900 spm (most sources) vs 1,050 spm (one review site). Catalog uses 900 [verify].
- Discontinued: 2020 per sewcanshe.com; PatternReview thread 113866 discussed the discontinuation. bernina.com still hosts the page. No Bernina successor; mechanicals moved to bernette; one dealer suggested the bernette 05 Academy (PatternReview review 6892).
