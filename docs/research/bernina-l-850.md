## Unit / naming checks

- Model number vs anything it implies: "L 850" is a series position, not a spec. L 850 is the mechanical-control air threader; L 860 adds a color touchscreen; L 890 adds chainstitch and coverstitch (5 thread). "Ultimate overlocker" is a flyer tagline.
- Throat measured needle-to-body or including the harp height? Dealer gives right-of-needle width and separate height; recorded as both.
- Speed: 1,500 spm on bernina.com and every dealer page.

## Owner themes (paraphrase + attribute)

1. Replacing a 20-year-old 800DL, an owner calls the L 850 substantial and steady at top speed; paid $2,499 on promotion and skipped the $1,500 step to the L 860. (owner: https://sewing.patternreview.com/review/machine/7332)
2. One unit shipped with a misaligned needle so the 4-thread stitch would not form; a chirp alert sounds when knee lift and pedal are used together; a tension issue traced to lifting the foot mid-seam. (owner: https://sewing.patternreview.com/review/machine/7238)
3. L 850 vs Baby Lock Acclaim: Bernina presets tension per stitch, Baby Lock adjusts continuously while sewing. (buyers: https://sewing.patternreview.com/SewingDiscussions/topic/121627)
