## Unit / naming checks

- Model number vs anything it implies: "TL-2000Qi" is not a 20 in arm. SMP's "Long-Arm" title is retailer language; arm is 9 in by dealer measure.
- Throat measured needle-to-body or including the harp height? Quality Sewing states 9 in "between the needle and the arm". Height not given.
- Speed: manufacturer max vs retailer copy: 1,500 spm in dealer copy; jukihome.com snippet did not show a number.
- Dimensions: one snippet mislabels 350 mm as 9 in height. 350 mm is 13.8 in. Recorded as converted from mm.

## Spec conflicts

- Weight: 37.8 lb (qualitysewing.com snippet, likely shipping weight) vs 25.4 lb / 11.5 kg (dsinternational.com, kenssewingcenter.com). Catalog will use 25.4 lb because it matches Juki's kg figure and the TL-2010Q sibling.
- Dimensions: conversion error in one snippet (see unit check). Catalog will use 17.8 x 8.6 x 13.8 in.
- Price: $899 (Michaels, World Weidner) vs $799 (eBay dealer) vs $1,699 historical SMP. Catalog will use $899.
- Included feet: no free motion or 1/4 in feet in the box (SMP list), unlike the TL-2010Q. Forum posters confirm.

## Owner themes (paraphrase + attribute)

1. Workhorse: PatternReview owners across 52 listed skill levels describe smooth running, excellent topstitching, and single out the knee lift and thread cutter. (owner: https://sewing.patternreview.com/SewingMachine/1572 ; reviews 7467, 7087, 5201)
2. Speed control: a discussion asks how to sew slower on the 2000Qi since there is no slider; answers cover pedal technique. (owner: https://sewing.patternreview.com/SewingDiscussions/topic/78012)
3. Minor gripes: Walmart reviewers (4.4 of 5) cite weak lighting, a small needle hole in the plate and presser foot drag in free motion with the foot down. (owner: https://www.walmart.com/reviews/product/154742741)
