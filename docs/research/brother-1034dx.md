## Owner themes (paraphrase + attribute)

1. Comparison reviewers describe the DX as the same machine as the 1034D with a brighter LED, deeper trim trap and dials moved to the right; whether that is worth the price gap is contested. (evidence: positioning; https://www.sewingmachinefun.com/brother-1034d-vs-1034dx/ ; https://sergerpro.com/brother-1034dx-review/)
2. Threading walkthroughs single out the lower looper path: route left and under the presser foot rather than to the right of the bar. (evidence: positioning; https://embroideryhoopstore.com/blogs/articles/easiest-way-to-thread-brother-1034dx-serger)
